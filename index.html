Words

One word per hit, in order. Hindi, Hinglish and English all work; Devanagari words switch to a face that has the script. Rate, size, stretch and glitch are in the Words dials.

Recipes save a setup

A recipe is the style, seed, every dial with its source and depth, the words and the font set. Your footage and song are not in it; load those and the recipe recreates the edit.
