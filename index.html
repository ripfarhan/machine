Clarity pulls the photo back through the effects: it tames warps, melts and palette strength without touching cuts or flicker.

Words

Off by default: nothing with words appears in any style until you switch Words on. On: each line is a phrase — it starts on a bar, plays its words in order on a steady grid (Word rate: bar / half-bar / beat) in one look, then rests. Hindi, Hinglish and English all work; Devanagari words switch to a face that has the script. Rate, size, stretch and glitch are in the Words dials.

Spread lines →

Sync: play the reel and press L when each line is sung — it stamps the next line at the playhead. Or type a time, nudge with ◀ ▶, or hit ● to set a line to the playhead. Times are reel seconds.

Recipes save a setup

Effects

Every sample is computed: FM operators, Karplus–Strong strings, modal resonators (membrane, bar, glass, metal), band-limited oscillators through state-variable filters, granular clouds, an 8-line feedback-delay reverb. The music comes from systems that evolve rather than loop — Markov harmony with voice leading, a motif that develops and mutates, cellular automata rewriting rhythms every bar, logistic-map and Lorenz-attractor modulation. Fifteen styles: four on the evolving engine (cinematic, broken beat/IDM, downtempo, ambient) and eleven on a genre engine (deep house, dub techno, techno, acid, electro, UK garage, drum & bass, lo-fi hip hop, trap, synthwave, amapiano), each with its own kit, groove library, bass line, chord and top-line roles, harmony loops and effect combinations. New piece changes the song (style, key, tempo, groove, sounds, effects) and keeps your dials; Randomize rolls everything inside one style; Variation keeps key, chords and sounds and rewrites grooves, lines and arrangement. Same seed + same dials = the same track.
