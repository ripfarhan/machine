# BARS: how the seven influences became genes

What is sourced and what is judgement. Written sources describe mood and instruments, rarely numbers, so the tempos, swing and
millisecond offsets in `js/beats/20-dna.js` are production judgement. They are not measurements of these records. To measure, drop
reference tracks in and run `tools/casestudy.py`.

| Artist | What the sources say | Turned into |
|---|---|---|
| Kendrick Lamar | Producers fit music to his lyrics; he often wrote over "a piano" or "a basic kick-snare rhythm"; band-style jams; processed vocal chops; ELEMENT went from too jazzy and four-on-the-floor to more bounce; loops reworked with reversed elements; a beat switch on a chosen bar ([Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/dj-dahi-breaks-down-the-five-year-process-behind-kendrick-lamars-new-album-1357269/), [The FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2017/04/24/sounwave-interview-kendrick-lamar-damn-track-explainer)) | Bounce, played jazz kit, hard minimal grooves; dissonant piano riff; funk bass; tenor sax; beat-switch scene |
| J. Cole | 80–100 BPM, kick and snare 10–30 ms late, 50–60 % swing on hats, rimshot + clap, Rhodes with 7ths and 9ths, soul loops, vinyl noise; verses strip back, hooks add layers (a type-beat guide: [Output](https://output.com/blog/j-cole-type-beat)) | Boom bap, late-snare pocket, 12-bit kit, pressed soul and gospel records, crackle, the verse/hook form |
| Smino | Monte Booker records objects (water bottle, pepper grinder, cracker box, clinking glasses) instead of kit sounds; stuttering arrhythmic kick; split-second chops; off-cadence delivery ([Bandcamp Daily](https://daily.bandcamp.com/monte-bookers-soulection-zero-fatigue-interview)) | Synthesised found-object kit, stuttering kick patterns, vocal chops, neo-soul chords (chords are my reading of his catalogue) |
| The Seige | LA duo: trap, epic lyricism, electronic anthemic hip-hop, primal drums, futuristic sound design; "future meets past" ([The Hype Magazine](https://www.thehypemagazine.com/2019/01/18/meet-hip-hop-powerhouse-duo-the-seige/), [press note](https://roster.trendpr.com/?p=975)). Assumed to be who "the siege" means | Stadium stomp, floor toms, stacked claps, saw riff, distorted bass |
| MC Stan | Slick trap verses, experimental production, Auto-Tune, wistful emo mood, long shapeshifting songs ([Tadipaar](https://rollingstoneindia.com/mc-stan-talks-debut-album-tadipaar-new-direction/amp), [Insaan](https://rollingstoneindia.com/mc-stan-insaan-album/amp)) | Trap with hat rolls, long gliding 808, sad plucked loop, bansuri (the flute is my choice) |
| Vijay DK | Mankhurd, Mumbai. Drill with hats, 808 slides and vocal samples; Marathi procession band pieces; gentle emotional tracks ([Indian Music Diaries](https://blog.theindianmusicdiaries.com/mumbai-rapper-vijay-dk-represents-hood-with-heart-on-sleeve-on-debut-album-4three4life)) | Drill grid, sliding 808, pressed procession band chopped, dhol and tasha, horn line |
| whysoKai | Hindi rap, introspective, literary; producer Aditya Pushkarna ([page](https://rapturemuse.com/songs/izhaar-lyrics-whysokai)). No written description of the beats was found | Weakest profile: sparse groove, felt piano, room for words. Inferred from the mood only |

Copyright: everything is synthesised from scratch and nothing is copied from these records. Chord loops are common idioms. "Pressed
records" are imaginary bands rendered by this code.
