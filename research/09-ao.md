# 09 — Max Cooper, "AO": reverse-engineering notes → the AO world

Measured from the user's file (4:39, 320 kbps MP3) with tools/casestudy.py (structure + basic-pitch transcription) and
hand analysis in numpy (FFT pitch of the sub, band envelopes, gate-rate autocorrelation, spectrograms). Everything in the
world (63-ao.js) is derived from a number here; nothing from the recording is reused.

## 1. The surprise: there are no drums
Every "percussive" onset is the same thing: the harmonic layer being amplitude-gated. Onset IOI histogram in every band
peaks at 1/8 of a beat (32nd notes) with a second mode at sextuplets; ~4–5 onsets per beat in the 2–9 kHz band, no
kick/snare/hat spectra, no transient tails. Beat tracker still locks (stability .998) because the gate is grid-locked.

## 2. Tempo, key, harmony
- 123.0 BPM, 4/4. G major (chroma key confidence .95).
- Bass fundamentals by FFT (not transcription): A2 110 Hz for the first ~95 s; then G1 49–50 Hz, B1 62 Hz, with
  C2 65 / D2 73 in the walking passage. Sections 48–110 s and 160 s sit ~+40 cents sharp (50.3 Hz vs 49.0 later).
- Three harmonic phases:
  1. **Hover (0–94 s)**: II pedal. A in the bass under D / Bm / Em / G clouds (notes D F# B E G). Unresolved for 1½ min.
  2. **Turn (94–125 s)**: the bass walks B → D → C → D (iii V IV V) — the only "chord changes" in the track.
  3. **Pendulum (125–265 s)**: IV ↔ I/3, i.e. C2 ↔ B1 in the bass alternating every 4 bars, with the 5th of the key (D4)
     held on top as a common tone through both chords (Cadd9 → G/B). Plagal rocking, no dominant.
- Transcription pitch classes: D 370, G 351, B 254, A 229, C 142 — the whole track lives on G–D–B with A/C as the tension.

## 3. The sub
- Sine-ish fundamental with 2nd/3rd harmonic (energy 100–300 Hz grows when the note is held), decay to −20 dB ≈ 270 ms.
- Rhythm (sub envelope on a 32nd grid, 100 s): peaks at 32nd positions 4/12/20/28 → the pulse sits on the off-beats
  ("and" of every beat), with a secondary hit a 16th before (3/11/19/27): a double hit. Downbeat gets the low octave,
  the off-beat pulses the octave above in the hover phase (A1 / A2 alternation in the transcription).
- The drop (175.8 s, the largest energy jump in the piece): the pulse STOPS and one note is held for ~7 s, distorted.

## 4. The cloud
- A sustained chord tone stack, rich harmonics 300 Hz–4 kHz (saw-like, filtered), horizontal lines that step with the
  harmony, chopped vertically by the gate.
- Gate rate by autocorrelation of the 1.5–8 kHz envelope in 4 s windows: sextuplets at 10–25 s, 32nds by 40 s, 64ths
  (≈16 Hz, near audio-rate buzz) from 85 s through the peaks, back to sextuplets/8ths at 250–265 s. The stutter
  accelerates over the piece — a process, not a loop.
- Stereo: side/mid 0.57 above 1.5 kHz (wide: the gate alternates channels), 0.15 below 150 Hz (sub mono).
- 228–262 s: everything above ~1 kHz removed (a closed low-pass), the sub keeps pulsing — the "dark" section.

## 5. Spectrum / dynamics
- Centroid 179 Hz, roll-off (85 %) 269 Hz, tilt −21.6 dB/decade. Band balance (dB): sub −3.6, bass −4.6, lowmid −9.1,
  mid −11.0, himid −20.4, high −28.5. Crest 6.9 dB, loudness range 4 dB: slammed and relentless.

## 6. What the world does with it
- `A.CloudInst`: a poly drone whose output is gated at a beat-locked rate (steps per beat 4/6/8/16), duty .5, partial
  ping-pong (pp .65 → side/mid 0.8 in the peak), with a ridden low-pass (5.2 k hover → 8 k peak → 520 Hz dark).
- `aosub` part: downbeat low octave + off-beat pulses (+1 octave while hovering), the 16th-early double hit 35 % of
  bars, the II pedal in the hover, the walk in the turn, IV root / I third in the pendulum, a 2-bar held note at
  75 % of peak starts.
- `aocloud` part: chord voicing + the key's 5th on top (common tone) + the root low (150–200 Hz body); rate 6 → 8 → 16
  by section; a rare high single tone late in the peak.
- Form `ao`: hover 32 → turn 16 (build) → pendulum 32 (peak) → dark 16 (break) → pendulum 24 (peak) → out 8.
- Family `ao`: IV(add9) · I · IV(add9) · iii|I, 4 bars each. Auto-EQ off (aeqFrom 2) so the darkness survives.
- Calibration (peak section, relative to the sub band): ours bass −5.4 / lowmid −10 / mid −9.7 / himid −18 / high −32
  vs the reference −1 / −5.5 / −7.4 / −17 / −25. Close in the mids; the sub's harmonics and the hiss are a few dB shy on
  purpose (the reference is a mastered stereo file, ours still goes through the user's macros).
